## Make prevention part of the routine

Heart health is built over time. Regular check-ups can help you and your care team understand your blood pressure, cholesterol and personal risk factors.

## Choose one change at a time

If it is appropriate for you, make room for movement during the week, choose a varied diet, avoid tobacco and ask for support with stress or sleep. Small steps that fit your life are easier to maintain.

## Ask for individual advice

Health needs differ from person to person. If you have a long-term condition, take regular medication, are pregnant or have new symptoms, speak with a qualified clinician before making changes. This article is general information and is not a diagnosis or treatment plan.
