## Start with a conversation

Your clinician may ask about your health history, current medicines and any concerns you would like to discuss. Bring a list of medicines and recent results if you have them.

## Tests are tailored to you

A wellness visit does not require the same tests for everyone. Your clinician will explain any recommended checks, why they may help and what the results mean.

## Make a plan together

Ask questions and let the care team know what matters to you. If you are unsure whether a package is suitable, contact the centre before booking. This general information does not replace individual medical advice.
